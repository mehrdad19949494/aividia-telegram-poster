### 🩺 Webapp 2: B2B GLP-1 Prescribing Assistant Suite v1.2 (Doctors & Specialists)
* **Live App URL:** `https://aividia.ir/glp1-prescribing-assistant-2/`
* **Target Audience:** Medical Doctors, General Practitioners, Endocrinologists, Internal Medicine Specialists, Obesity & Aesthetic Clinics.
* **Lead Generation Funnel:**
  1. **Clinical Decision Support Hook:** Posts highlighting complex titration schedules, nausea/GI management, and drug-switching protocols (e.g. Semaglutide to Tirzepatide).

   - All text, labels, step numbers, titles, and callouts baked into generated images MUST be in **Persian (فارسی)**.

2. **Medical Practitioners & Doctors (B2B Audience):**
   - Text inside clinical titration ladders, pharmacology diagrams, adverse effect tables, and dosing charts is **allowed and preferred in English** (international medical standards) or bilingual.

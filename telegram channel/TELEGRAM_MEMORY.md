## 6. VISUAL ASSET & INFOGRAPHIC LANGUAGE PROTOCOL

When generating images, diagrams, infographics, checklists, or titration charts for Telegram and content channels:

1. **For General Population (B2C Audiences):**
   - **Rule:** Any embedded text, titles, annotations, symptom labels, or step numbers inside generated graphics MUST be in **Persian (فارسی)**.
   - **Typography & Clarity:** Use clean Persian text, high contrast, RTL flow, and legible labels (e.g. *«علائم هشداردهنده خال»*, *«مراحل ترمیم سد دفاعی پوست»*).
   - **Tone:** Consumer-friendly, educational, reassuring, and scientifically grounded.

2. **For Doctors & Medical Specialists (B2B Audiences):**
   - **Rule:** Embedded text inside clinical decision charts, titration ladders, pharmacology diagrams, adverse reaction matrices, and drug-switching protocols is **allowed and preferred in English** (international medical standard) or bilingual (Persian + English Latin medical terms).
   - **Medical Terminology:** Latin international drug names (*Tirzepatide, Semaglutide, Liraglutide, Retatrutide*), clinical units (*mg/week, subcutaneous*), adverse reactions (*Nausea, Pancreatitis, Gastroparesis, Gallbladder*), and guideline citations.
